No. 1 · South Floe Edition — Two Herring

Herring Futures Collapse After Colony Learns To Share

Market analysts baffled as generosity proves structurally incompatible with speculation.

Trading on the South Floe exchange halted twice before noon yesterday after a juvenile penguin, name withheld, handed a herring to a neighbour without demanding one in return. The gesture, described by one broker as "economically irresponsible", triggered a cascade of similar behaviour across three rookeries.

By dusk the price of a herring had fallen from four herring to approximately one herring, a figure economists concede is difficult to interpret. The exchange has suspended trading pending clarification of what a herring is now worth in herring.

Elder penguins have urged calm, noting that the colony survived the Great Krill Correction of two winters ago by the simple expedient of eating the disputed asset. That approach is again under consideration.

A spokesbird for the exchange said the floor would reopen once someone could explain the situation slowly and with fish.
